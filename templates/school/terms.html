School Policies & Guidelines

Terms & Conditions

Welcome to Pahilo Paila Montessori School (पहिलो पाइला Montessori School), located in Suda, Mahendranagar. These terms outline our admissions policies, student safety standards, and parent partnerships.

1. Admission & Enrollment Policies

Admissions to Pahilo Paila Montessori School (Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, and UKG) are granted based on age readiness, developmental assessment, and seat availability within each cohort. We maintain Dr. Maria Montessori's recommended educator-to-child ratios to guarantee personalized care and sensorial learning.

  • • Age milestones must be met by the child prior to commencing the respective level.
  • • An initial parent observation session and child orientation are required before final placement.
  • • Official birth registration copies and immunization records must be submitted at the time of admission.

2. School Attendance & Pick-up Safety

Child security is our highest priority. School operating hours run Sunday through Friday. Children will only be released to designated parents, guardians, or authorized family representatives who carry official school identification.

  • • Arrival and pick-up must strictly follow cohort schedule times.
  • • In the event of an emergency pickup by a non-registered guardian, prior telephonic confirmation with the administrative office (984-8636068) is mandatory.

3. Health, Nutrition & Hygiene Guidelines

To protect the health of all children, parents must notify the school if their child exhibits fever, infectious conditions, or acute symptoms. Children recovering from contagious illnesses may return after full recovery or with medical clearance.

  • • Healthy, hygienic, age-tailored snacks are provided within school hours.
  • • Any dietary restrictions, allergies, or special physical requirements must be stated in the child's admission profile.

4. Media, Photographs & Activities

Photographs and short video documentation of classroom sensorial exercises, science exploration, and school events are maintained for pedagogical portfolios and official school communication on our verified Facebook page. Parents wishing to opt out may state so upon admission registration.
